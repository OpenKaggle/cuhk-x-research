# CUHK-X Small Model Track research

This directory preserves first-party source and detailed research evidence for
the lightweight multimodal HAR track. It is a research archive, not a
redistributable data or model package.

## What is here

- `src/` — first-party boundary checks, extraction utilities, and training
  harnesses. They accept independently acquired, authorized inputs only.
- `config/` — sanitised public configuration records: budgets, protocols,
  provenance references, and gate decisions without subject or record lists.
- `receipts/` — participant-authored process and validation records with
  contact details, local paths, and participant-index literals removed.

## Not included

No competition archive, copied organizer source, public-mirror payload,
trained checkpoint, generated submission, or local environment appears here.
Organizer material and public notebooks are linked from
[UPSTREAMS.md](../UPSTREAMS.md), not re-hosted.

The root [release manifest](../RELEASE_MANIFEST.md) defines the complete
publication boundary.
