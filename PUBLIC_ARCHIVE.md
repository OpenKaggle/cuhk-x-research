# Public archive scope

This is a public source-and-evidence archive, not a mirror of a competition
workspace. The Large and Small tracks remain separate directories because
their methods and constraints differ; their shared publication boundary is
defined by [RELEASE_MANIFEST.md](RELEASE_MANIFEST.md).

Included are first-party source, tests, reproducibility protocols, detailed
derived validation evidence, hashes, version identifiers, and public-outcome
summaries. Excluded material is linked to an official source where appropriate
rather than copied here.

For the policy and its file-level application, read
[PUBLIC_RELEASE_AUDIT.md](PUBLIC_RELEASE_AUDIT.md). For related work, see the
[OpenKaggle portfolio](https://github.com/OpenKaggle/kaggle-research-portfolio).
